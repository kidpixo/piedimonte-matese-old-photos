**The Egg Cotton Mill in Piedimonte Matese: An Industrial and Social History Through Maps**

Welcome to the site dedicated to the history of the Egg Cotonificio of Piedimonte Matese, a company that for over a century has profoundly influenced the economic and social life of this area.
From its origins at the beginning of the 19th century until its destruction in 1943, the story of the Egg Cotton Mill represents a significant page in the industrial and social history of southern Italy, both in the pre-unification and post-unification era.

The long history of the Egg Cotton Mill, which lasted about 130 years, ended tragically on 19 October 1943, when the plant was destroyed by German sappers.
Today, where the imposing four-story building once stood, stands Piazza Carmine.

This site aims to keep the memory of this important industrial reality alive, exploring its history through documents, testimonies and, in particular, historical maps.

The work of georeferencing these ancient documents, such as those taken from the precious book "The Egg cotton mill of Piedimonte d'Alife" by the Gruppo Memorie Storiche and the 1964 map found by Daniela Mastrolorenzo, presents challenges related to reference systems and anchoring points, but offers a unique opportunity to rediscover and enhance this important heritage.

## Explore the Website

This interactive database offers several ways to discover the history of the Cotton Mill:

### [📷 Photos]({{ '/photos/' | relative_url }})
A complete collection of georeferenced historical images, presented in a searchable and sortable tabular format. Discover rare and previously unpublished photos of the cotton mill, cityscape and community life over the last two centuries. Each photo is accompanied by historical metadata and is linked to related themes.

### [🗺️ General Map]({{ '/map/' | relative_url }})
An interactive geospatial visualization showing photo locations, historical viewpoints, fields of view, and documented routes. Perfect for understanding how the Piedimonte Matese area has changed over time and for locating the historical sites linked to the cotton mill.

### [🏷️ Labels]({{ '/labels/' | relative_url }})
Browse the collection by theme: architecture, war damage, demolitions, characters, historical trades and more. Filter photos by topic of interest and discover how different aspects of the cotton mill's history are interconnected.

### [📝 Topics]({{ '/topics/' | relative_url }})
Edited articles and essays on the history of the cotton mill, based on testimonies, archival documents and primary sources. Thematic insights that link the photos to historical and social contexts, written by researchers and custodians of local memory.
